# CanopyOps v0.3.0

CanopyOps now opens on a visual crop workspace: named rooms and zones, evidence through time, compatible measurement plots and observation pair comparisons. The interface makes spatial and temporal context persistent while retaining the native Markdown/CSV library, capture, photos, import originals, history and conflict-aware saves.

Understory, Lampblack and Clearspan are distinct working environments with the same evidence: botanical overview, darkroom source inspection and diffused greenhouse daylight. Generated atmosphere artwork is decorative and conveys no crop finding.

The September 30 same-version repair replaces the Sodium and Sunprint presentations with Lampblack and Clearspan. It also corrects crop-walk photo capture so a stale observation cannot leave an orphan photo, and rejects malformed photo data and non-finite measurements. The evidence and record workflows remain the v0.3.0 promise.

This is an update from the accepted portable v0.2.0 promise. The new exploration and comparison workflows expand the product beyond its record desk. The repository-native v0.1.5 plugin remains a separate distribution line.

The workspace runs locally with Python 3.10+ and a browser. It does not connect sensors, interpolate crop conditions, approve targets or control equipment. Local browser/package verification is recorded separately; macOS, fresh customer hosts and field outcomes remain unverified.
