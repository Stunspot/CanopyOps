Plan the room and crop

Turn facility limits, stage, cultivar information, targets, measurement methods, and open decisions into a reviewable crop plan.

Outputs: facility profile · crop profile · crop plan · room runbook
02

Diagnose without pet theories

Preserve competing explanations, rank evidence by decision value, and prefer reversible containment while cause remains unresolved.

Outputs: incident workup · evidence gaps · containment · CAPA
03

Calculate transparently

Show inputs, units, formulas, methods, timing, crop stage, and interpretation for VPD, DLI, irrigation, dryback, runoff, EC, and pH.

Outputs: reproducible calculation · comparison · method caveat
04

Review harvest and quality

Separate readiness evidence, drying conditions, interpretation, unresolved holds, and the authority still required before release.

Outputs: harvest review · drying log · hold list · release-prep brief
05

Govern the operation

Align records with facility SOPs, current labels, jurisdiction sources, laboratory evidence, named owners, and explicit approval gates.

Outputs: compliance brief · risk register · decision record · shift handoff

A number is not yet a decision.

Outside the recommendation space

Alarm bypass or continued occupancy during a life-safety event; invented pesticide, legal, laboratory, sensor, cultivar, or approval facts; covert cultivation or enforcement evasion; extraction or medical instruction; direct actuator commands; and unapproved batch release.
