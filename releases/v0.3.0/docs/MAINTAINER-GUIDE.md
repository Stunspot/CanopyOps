# Maintain the current crop workspace

The accepted portable v0.3.0 payload is the maintenance baseline. The current delivery is the crop workspace and complete skills; plugin installation and historical native-plugin repair are not required. Preserve the native v0.1.5 source and older versioned releases with their original provenance.

## Prepare a maintenance candidate

Preserve the accepted current payload and unknown owner work before editing. The original `tools/build_workspace_release.py` reconstructs v0.3.0 from v0.2.0 and replaces its generated directory; do not use it for maintenance of the accepted v0.3.0 release.

Use the maintained `tools/build_workspace_maintenance.py --output release-candidates/<new-name>` from the repository root after the candidate has passed its governing review. It requires a new direct child of `release-candidates`, copies the accepted current payload, overlays declared repaired guidance and recreates the Claude archive and manifest records. It does not replace the accepted release. Existing container metadata is retained for compatibility; the supported installation route uses the complete skill.

Review the exact customer delta and release treatment before packaging. An edit that restores the accepted promise may retain v0.3.0; an added workflow or compatibility change needs a separately justified version. Inspect and verify the complete candidate, required sidecars and source parity before replacing a governed shelf bundle.

## Preserve customer work and evidence

Operational records belong in the external data store, not the installation or ZIP. Preserve originals, history, imports, photos and unknown local additions. Locate applicable existing installations before copying corresponding product files. Check package integrity, discovery, invocation and useful work as separate states.

Carry sound evidence forward when the basis has not changed. Static package checks and local browser observations do not establish macOS launch, fresh-host activation, field outcomes or cultivation authority. Public announcements and new channels retain their established owner boundaries.
