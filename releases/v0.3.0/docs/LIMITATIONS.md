# CanopyOps: boundaries

CanopyOps uses supplied evidence. A symptom pattern or photograph is not a confirmed diagnosis. A supplied target is a comparison value until its stage, scope, tolerance, source and approval establish applicable authority. Consequential crop, pesticide, worker-safety, compliance and release decisions require the appropriate accountable human.

The workspace's rooms and zones are an observation index. Their positions do not establish physical geometry or surveyed distance. Recorded measurements remain discrete observations with their original context; a plot is not a sensor connection or interpolated atmospheric field. Through-date views cannot establish the condition of a room now. Missing observations do not establish crop health.

The workspace supports its native crop-walk CSV and text records. Arbitrary documents do not become structured measurements automatically. Native text remains available where a custom format does not match the capture contract. Decorative atmosphere artwork and illustrative examples are not evidence from the user's crop.

The service binds to this computer's loopback interface. It does not operate equipment, connect sensors or provide a network phone service. Python 3.10+ is required for the workspace. Records, imports, history and photos live in an external store and need a separate backup.

Windows/browser and static-package checks have bounded local evidence. macOS launch, fresh customer hosts, Claude upload/activation, field fitness, regulatory coverage and crop outcomes remain unverified unless separately observed. See [host evidence](HOST-EVIDENCE-BOUNDARY.md).
