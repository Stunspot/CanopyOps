# Support

CanopyOps is a free Collaborative Dynamics Augment. Support is best-effort and routed through the public repository.

Read [`RELEASE-STATUS.md`](RELEASE-STATUS.md) before reporting a problem. State whether the issue affects the repository-native v0.1.5 line, the portable v0.1.6 bundle, or both.

## Use the right issue type

Open [CanopyOps Issues](https://github.com/Stunspot/CanopyOps/issues/new/choose) and choose the closest template:

- **Bug report** — installation, packaging, broken links, incorrect deterministic output, missing files, version selection, or behavior that contradicts the documented workflow.
- **Source-currency concern** — a legal, regulatory, label, safety, laboratory, horticultural, or equipment claim may be stale, mis-scoped, or weakly sourced.
- **Capability request** — a proposed workflow, artifact, calculation, adapter, or quality-of-life improvement.
- **Security report** — use the private route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

## Include a useful evidence bundle

For software, package, installation, or documentation defects, include:

- CanopyOps distribution line and version;
- installation method and exact artifact path;
- host, host version, and operating system;
- exact command or request used;
- expected and observed result;
- whether package validation, discovery, explicit invocation, resource loading, and tool execution succeeded;
- a minimal fictional or sanitized reproduction;
- relevant error text or checksum values.

For source-currency concerns, include the affected file and claim, jurisdiction or scope, date checked, and a current primary source when available.

Do not report “it failed” without identifying which state failed. A valid ZIP, an installed plugin, a discovered skill, an invoked workflow, and healthy deterministic tools are different observations.

## Keep sensitive information out of public issues

Do not post facility addresses, access details, employee or customer data, licence identifiers, security controls, proprietary recipes, private logs, API keys, credentials, precise inventory, or unredacted compliance records.

Reduce the problem to fictional or sanitized facts. If that is impossible, request a private route through https://collaborative-dynamics.com before transmitting material.

## Not an emergency or professional-help channel

Repository support is not appropriate for:

- active CO2 or life-safety alarms;
- worker exposure, fire, electrical, gas, HVAC, or structural hazards;
- crop emergencies requiring immediate qualified action;
- legal deadlines or regulator instructions;
- medical questions;
- pesticide authorization;
- batch-release decisions;
- direct equipment operation.

Use the facility emergency procedure and the appropriate qualified responder, regulator, attorney, laboratory, safety professional, equipment specialist, or accountable operator.

## Response expectations

An issue may be acknowledged, reproduced, requested for clarification, deferred, declined, or incorporated into a future canonical release. Opening an issue does not create a service-level agreement, consulting relationship, operational approval, compensation obligation, or promise of a fix.

Historical artifacts may be preserved even when a newer distribution exists. Support will not silently rewrite old evidence to make it appear that a later package was tested.
