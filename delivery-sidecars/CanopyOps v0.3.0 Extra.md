# Description

CanopyOps provides evidence-led cannabis cultivation operations guidance for real crops and facilities. It uses measurements, symptoms, crop stage, recent changes, environmental conditions, and operating constraints to diagnose problems and compare next steps across climate, irrigation, nutrition, lighting, crop steering, pest pressure, workflow, and production safety. Advice remains specific to the applicable jurisdiction and facility rather than relying on generic grow lore. The visual crop workspace makes supplied observations easier to compare across rooms, named zones and time. Follow compatible measurements, place two observations alongside their methods and evidence, inspect photo references, capture the next crop walk, and carry incidents or shift handoffs into the native record library. Understory, Lampblack and Clearspan give the same evidence three distinct working environments: botanical overview, darkroom source inspection and diffused greenhouse daylight. Native Markdown and CSV remain shared with your agent, with preserved imports and changed-record warnings. It is a local evidence workspace; it does not connect sensors or operate equipment.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from "Additional Files" to your Codex, Claude Code, or other harness, or add the .zip to a project knowledge base in Chat. Attach or reference the .zip in chat and say, "Install this Augment." The prompt in this post is optional.

- Start with your jurisdiction, crop and growth stage, facility or environment, exact symptoms or decision, current measurements, and anything that recently changed.
- Confirm local law, licenses, labels, and workplace policy before acting. Use qualified professionals for electrical, fire, chemical, structural, respiratory, biological, or worker-safety hazards.
- CanopyOps recommends and explains; it does not operate equipment, alter schedules, or change production systems on its own.

Public GitHub Repo: [CanopyOps](https://github.com/Stunspot/CanopyOps)

- Ask “Open CanopyOps,” or use the own-location Windows/macOS launcher in the workspace folder; Python 3.10+ is required.
- Keep your native records outside the installation. The default store is Documents/CanopyOps; CANOPYOPS_DATA_HOME selects another external folder.
- Choose a room and zone, revisit the evidence through a date, and compare observations with their timestamps, units, method and tool. Missing context stays unknown.
- Record observations and photo references; reconcile changed records before saving. Desktop shortcuts are optional, never silently installed.
